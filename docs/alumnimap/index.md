# Alumni Map

Career Diversity and Summer Bridge fellows have accepted positions across the United States, and beyond! The map below shows where alumni who either participated in the Career Diversity workshop or in the Summer Bridge program at the University of Illinois Urbana Champaign are working. From the Bar Foundation to non-profit grant making to research administration to professorships, HWW alumni are using their skills in broad variety of contexts and demonstrating that there is no singular trajectory to be followed in the PhD journey.
